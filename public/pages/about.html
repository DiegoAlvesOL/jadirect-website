About JA Direct

Logistics built on trust, reliability and results.

Who We Are

JA Direct was founded over 10 years ago by Johnny Ahearne with a simple belief: that businesses across Ireland deserve a logistics partner they can truly rely on. What started as a small operation in Dublin has grown into a trusted delivery service connecting companies to their customers, quickly, efficiently and with care.

Our Mission

Our mission is straightforward. We connect businesses to the people they serve, making sure every collection and every delivery happens on time, every time. We do this by investing in our team, maintaining our fleet to the highest standards and treating every shipment as if it were our own.

Our Values

We believe that great logistics is built on three things: reliability, respect and responsibility. Reliability for our clients, who count on us every day. Respect for our drivers and support team, who make it all happen. And responsibility toward the environment, which is why our vehicles undergo regular walkaround checks and maintenance to keep them running cleanly and safely across Ireland.

Ireland Is Our Home

We operate exclusively across Ireland and that focus is intentional. Knowing every county, every route and every client personally is what sets us apart from larger operators. When you work with JA Direct, you are not a reference number. You are a partner.
